**Added (ground floor)**
- A low open cabinet against the back wall, right of the counter, with a **CRT TV** on top (generic pixel-art screen, rabbit ears, a soft cyan glow on the floor).
- Three **unbranded consoles** in the cabinet's cubbies, told apart by shape and colour only: a chunky dark box with a green LED (stands in for the Xbox), a flat black slab with a blue LED (the PS2) and a slim upright white box with a glowing slot (the Wii). - A low **coffee table** in front with an open clamshell **handheld** on it (the DS), on a small round rug, with one pouf beside it.

## Verification
Type-checked with the stub-typed `tsc` (exit 0). Rendered through the right-hand shop window and from inside in software WebGL. Not tested: the real Vite build, the Vercel deploy, phones, GPU frame rate.

## Notes
- The consoles carry no logos or lettering and are not copies of the real product designs, in line with the project rule (evoke, do not copy). If you want them to read more clearly as the real ones, the route is your own photos of them on a poster or frame.
- The pouf is an extra so the corner has somewhere to sit; say so if you want it gone.
- The round table in the shop window partly hides the cabinet's lower half from the street. It shows fully from a higher camera angle or from inside.
