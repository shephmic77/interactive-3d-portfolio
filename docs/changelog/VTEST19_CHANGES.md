# vtest19: sparser climbing wall, more hold types, a V4-5 problem

Changed file: `src/world.ts`.

**Less rock**
- The coral route is now about half as many holds, the cyan and pink problems are down to a handful each, and the neutral foot chips are about a third as many. The lowest section is no longer crowded.

**More hold types** (besides jugs, slopers, crimps, pinches and chips)
- Pockets (round block with a dark finger hole), edge rails (pentagonal, some in plywood colour), horns (cones that stick straight out) and triangular prism ridges.
- The violet volume moved up to the middle of the wall so the hard problem can use space around it.

**A hard V4-5 problem, in gold**
- Two taped starts, a pinch and a sloper, then a long reach to a crimp, a lock-off to a side pocket, a rail, a dyno from a sloper to a horn, and a finish jug. Gold foot chips, taped start and finish, and a "V4-5" tag at the start.
- The coral line is now a V0 warm-up, cyan is V2 and pink is V3. These grade tags are decoration I made up, not real grades, and the problem is sketched, not set by a route-setter.
