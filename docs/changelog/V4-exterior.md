# V4: exterior and lighting

## Design boundary

The underlying architecture remains **a wide ground floor + one smaller rear-left upper floor + open terraces**. No additional enclosed storeys or personal memorabilia have been added. Details are functional, ordinary exterior features; the personally meaningful art/collectibles in `ENDGAME_ONLY.md` remain untouched.

## Ground-floor storefront
- Glass display windows with textured bookshelves behind them; variation includes planter boxes, partially closed blinds, and dark panes.
- Window headers, sills, masonry courses, awning mounting brackets, door threshold and kick plate.
- A bench for waiting, serviceable entrance lamps, compact support crates, existing dedicated book cart and resume stand.

## Exposed middle terrace
- Two outdoor chairs with table in the wide right-side space, placed away from its entrance.
- Railing planters and trailing greenery, wall lamp beside the terrace door.
- Low-point drain and downspout connected to the ground-level drainage.

## Upper rooftop terrace
- Outdoor seating beside a bistro table, low railings, plants and warm festoon bulbs.
- Mechanical area: tank, condenser, ventilator, utility locker, antenna, a connected outlet line.

## Side and back elevations
- Two bracket-mounted HVAC units with visible fan grilles.
- Slatted vents, utility cabinets, power conduits, wall-mounted lights and rainwater pipes.
- Back service hatch and maintenance ladder connecting the service access to rooftop machinery.
- Contacts vending machine and Credits arcade remain next to each other behind the building, unobstructed.

## Light and performance
- Reduced broad ambient and global directional lighting; stronger **local practical lighting** from warm windows, amber lamps, signpost, vending machine and arcade.
- Preserved reduced-motion preference support and limited global glow animation.
- Procedural geometry with no externally downloaded textures or models. The lighting is a real-time approximation, not baked-GI photorealism.

## Installation
Unzip the files directly into a new folder, open that folder in VS Code, then run `npm install` and `npm run dev`. The ZIP deliberately puts `package.json` at the top level.
